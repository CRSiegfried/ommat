Mouse

Drag a body to move it. Drag empty space to pan (2D) or turn (3D); in 3D shift-, right-, middle- or space-drag slides the view instead. The wheel zooms about the pointer. Click selects; click again steps to the next body beneath. Double-click empty space to fit.

Trackpad and touch

A two-finger scroll or a pinch zooms; drag to pan or turn. Tap selects; drag a body to move it.

Reading a turn

A body keeps its kind fill; its ring is its entry: green held and clear of its bounds, amber short of one or Unsure, red Unavailable, a thin grey ring determined with no bound to clear, dashed Unasked or absent. The mark inside says the same: ✓ clear, ! short, ? Unsure, ✕ Unavailable, – Unasked. A target holds no entry; its ring is derived from the answers that estimate it. The policy wears an ink ring. The lineage is lit, its arrows pointing the way the information flowed, toward the policy; its root, the entry it rests on, wears a notched ring in the lineage colour. A hop named by a marker is thin; a declared read is dashed and is not a record of what was read. How far an answer lies from a bound is not how far the decision lies from changing.

Filters

The legend's kinds and the turn's entry and target chips are filters: press one to dim every other body (a body must match both a kind and a state when both are pressed), press again to clear.

Keys

f fit all · s fit the selection · h home · 3 3D · l lineage only · / find · a d previous / next round · . , walk the bodies, Enter selects · + - zoom · arrows turn or pan with the canvas focused (shift pans) · ← → j k step turns elsewhere · ? this help. Keys act on the canvas or with nothing focused, never on a focused button or field.

Esc clears one thing at a time: this help, the search, the selection, the state, target and kind filters, the round pinned. It never leaves the turn: the turn menu's "design alone" does that.
