layout
Mouse

Drag a body to move it. Drag empty space to pan (2D) or turn (3D); in 3D shift-, right-, middle- or space-drag slides the view instead. The wheel zooms. Click selects; click again steps to the next body beneath. Double-click empty space to fit.

Touch

Tap selects. Drag a body to move it; drag empty space to pan or turn. Two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.

Keys

f fit · s fit selection · h home · 3 3D · / find · a d previous / next round · + - zoom · arrows turn or pan with the canvas focused (shift pans) · ? this help

Esc clears one thing at a time: this help, the search, the selection, the round pinned, then backs out as on every tab.
